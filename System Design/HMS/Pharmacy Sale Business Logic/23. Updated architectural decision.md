### Updated architectural decision:
```git title:"Updated architecture"
**Sale UOM strategy (locked 2026-07-29, via AskUserQuestion, refined same day)**: Sale is conducted ONLY in `Drug.DispensingUOM` — no Box/Strip/Tablet multi-tier selection at sale time. Rationale: `Drug` only models a 2-tier UOM chain (`RequestUOM` ↔ `DispensingUOM` via `RequestCF`, for procurement), no third "Strip" tier exists; `PharmacyItemStock.QuantityStock` will be tracked in one canonical unit (`DispensingUOM`) to keep the atomic-deduction merge-key math unambiguous. Multi-UOM selling (Box/Strip retail sales) is explicitly deferred as a separate, bigger future feature requiring a new UOM-tier + second conversion factor on `Drug`.

  

**Important refinement on HOW to enforce this (user correction, 2026-07-29): do NOT remove or structurally lock `SalePharmacyItem.MeasurementUnitId`.** Keep it as a real field/FK relationship (still a `GeneralConfig` picker). Enforce "must equal `Drug.DispensingUOM`" purely as a **business-rule validation** in the UI (e.g. dropdown pre-filtered/pre-selected to the Drug's DispensingUOM) and backend service layer (reject if `MeasurementUnitId` resolves to something other than the Drug's DispensingUOM) — NOT by deleting the field, hardcoding it away, or any structural change that would make relaxing the rule later hard. Explicit design intent: "Canonical Stock Unit হবে DispensingUOM, কিন্তু Design ভবিষ্যতের Extension-এর জন্য উন্মুক্ত থাকবে" (canonical stock unit is DispensingUOM, but the design stays open for future extension) — when multi-UOM Sale is eventually built, it should only require loosening this validation rule, not restructuring the field.
```

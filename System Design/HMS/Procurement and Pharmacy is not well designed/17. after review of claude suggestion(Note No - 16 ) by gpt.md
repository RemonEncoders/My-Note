# Final Opinion

আমি Claude-কে এই উত্তর দিতাম:

> I agree with introducing `PublishedProductLink` as a neutral correlation table and making `IProductPublisher` generic. I think those changes improve the architecture significantly.
> 
> However, I would keep `PublishedAt`, `PublishedBy`, `DiscontinuedAt`, and `DiscontinuedBy` on the `Drug` aggregate.
> 
> My reasoning is that `PublishedProductLink` should remain a pure integration artifact whose responsibility is only to correlate a source entity with a shared `Product`.
> 
> The publishing history, on the other hand, is part of the `Drug` aggregate's own business lifecycle. An aggregate should be able to answer questions about itself ("When was I published?", "Who published me?", "Have I been discontinued?") without depending on an external correlation table.
> 
> Therefore I would keep the responsibilities separated:
> 
> - **Drug** owns lifecycle and audit.
>     
> - **PublishedProductLink** owns cross-module identity mapping.
>     
> - **Product** remains a pure procurement/inventory entity.
>     
> 
> This keeps each object focused on a single responsibility while avoiding unnecessary coupling between business state and integration infrastructure.

## আমার মতে এটাই final architecture

এখন আমি আর বড় কোনো architectural সমস্যা দেখছি না।

এটা bounded context, DDD, modular monolith এবং enterprise integration—সব দিক থেকেই একটি শক্তিশালী এবং maintainable design। এরপর design document freeze করে implementation-এ যাওয়া যুক্তিযুক্ত।

## আমার Recommendation

আমি Claude-কে এভাবে উত্তর দিতাম:

> I agree with the derived-state approach, but I would stop before introducing `ApprovedAt` and `ApprovedBy`.
> 
> My concern is that "Approved" does not have a clear domain owner. Clinical approval is already represented by `FormularyStatus = Active`, while procurement approval belongs to SCM, not PHAR.
> 
> Following the DDD principle that every state should represent a distinct business process with a clear owner and transition rules, I would keep the integration lifecycle limited to:
> 
> - Never Published
>     
> - Published
>     
> - Discontinued
>     
> 
> derived from `ProductId`, `PublishedAt`, and `DiscontinuedAt` (or equivalent audit information).
> 
> If a future business requirement introduces a real procurement approval workflow, I would integrate with the SCM approval engine rather than adding another state machine inside the Drug aggregate.

---

**এটাই আমি long-term maintainable design মনে করি।**

কারণ তুমি ইতিমধ্যেই `FormularyStatus` নামে একটি state machine maintain করছ। এখন একই Entity-তে আরেকটি approval state machine ঢুকালে aggregate অপ্রয়োজনীয়ভাবে জটিল হয়ে যাবে।

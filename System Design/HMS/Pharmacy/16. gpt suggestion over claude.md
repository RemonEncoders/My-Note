# আমার Final Recommendation

আমি Claude-কে বলতাম:

> **Approved.**
> 
> Add the `IsCentralWarehouse` flag and migrate away from all name-based Central Warehouse detection.
> 
> Keep `WarehouseModuleMapping` responsible only for module ownership (Pharmacy, OT, ICU, etc.).
> 
> Keep `IsCentralWarehouse` responsible only for identifying the hospital's central receiving warehouse.
> 
> These two concepts represent different business rules and should remain independent.

# Business Decision (Final)

After reviewing the current architecture, we have decided on the following business design.

This is the accepted architecture and should be treated as the target implementation.

## Central Warehouse Responsibility

The IMS module is responsible only for the **Central Warehouse**.

Its responsibilities include:

- Goods Receive
    
- Central Warehouse stock
    
- Inventory Request approval
    
- Inventory Transfer
    
- Central Warehouse inventory management
    

IMS is **NOT** responsible for managing Pharmacy stock after the transfer is completed.

---

## Department Responsibility

Each department owns and manages its own local inventory.

For example:

- Pharmacy → Pharmacy Store
    
- OT → OT Store
    
- ICU → ICU Store
    
- Laboratory → Laboratory Store
    
- Ward → Ward Store
    

Once inventory is transferred to a department, that department becomes the owner of the inventory.

---

## Pharmacy Responsibility

The Pharmacy module is responsible for:

- Pharmacy stock
    
- Batch management
    
- Expiry management
    
- Drug dispensing
    
- Pharmacy sale
    
- Patient drug issue
    
- Pharmacy costing
    
- Pharmacy-specific inventory
    

The Pharmacy module should continue using its own stock tables and business logic.

We are NOT planning to remove `phar_item_stocks`.

---

# Expected Business Workflow

Supplier  
→ Purchase Order  
→ Goods Receive  
→ Central Warehouse (IMS)  
→ Inventory Request (Pharmacy)  
→ Approval  
→ Inventory Transfer  
→ Pharmacy Store  
→ Pharmacy Sale

---

# Current Problem

Based on the investigation, Inventory Transfer successfully updates the IMS inventory.

However, after the transfer is completed, the Pharmacy module is not automatically updated.

As a result:

- Inventory has physically moved.
    
- IMS considers the transfer completed.
    
- Pharmacy does not receive the stock in its own inventory.
    
- Therefore Pharmacy cannot sell the transferred drugs.
    

This is the integration gap that must be solved.

---

# Expected Solution

After Inventory Transfer is completed and the destination is a Pharmacy Store,

the system should automatically notify the Pharmacy module.

The Pharmacy module should then:

- Create or update Pharmacy stock.
    
- Preserve batch information.
    
- Preserve expiry information.
    
- Preserve cost information.
    
- Preserve pharmacy-specific business rules.
    
- Make the transferred quantity immediately available for Pharmacy Sale.
    

IMS should not continue managing that transferred inventory.

Ownership has already been transferred to the Pharmacy.

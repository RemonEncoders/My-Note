# আমার Recommendation

আমি **Source Restrict করব**, কিন্তু **Destination Restrict করব না**।

অর্থাৎ:

## ✅ Source Warehouse

শুধু

- Central Warehouse
    
- Generic SCM Warehouse (OwnerModule নেই)
    

Allowed হবে।

```text
Central Warehouse
        │
        ▼
Inventory Request
        ▼
Source Warehouse
```

এখানে Department Warehouse (Pharmacy, OT, ICU...) কখনো Source হতে পারবে না।

এখানে আমি Restriction দেব না।


# আমি Claude-কে কী বলতাম?
```cmd title:"claude command"
I agree with your analysis.

I would like to adopt the following business rules.

## Source Warehouse

Restrict the Source Warehouse to **generic SCM warehouses only**.

This means:

- Central Warehouse (`IsCentralWarehouse = true`)
    
- Other warehouses with **no OwnerModule mapping**
    

Department-owned warehouses (Pharmacy, OT, ICU, Laboratory, Emergency, etc.) must never be selectable as the Source Warehouse.

This restriction should be enforced both:

- in the UI (dropdown filtering), and
    
- on the server side (validation in the service layer), including Transfer Confirm to prevent mapping drift.
    

## Destination Warehouse

For now, I prefer **not** to restrict the Destination Warehouse.

Keep the Destination dropdown flexible so that future workflows (additional generic warehouses, new operational scenarios, or future business requirements) do not require redesigning the request engine.

The current business process will naturally use department-owned warehouses as destinations, but I don't want to hard-code that restriction into the engine unless there is a clear business requirement.

Please update the implementation plan based on this decision and let me review it before writing any code.
```

## কেন আমি Destination Restrict করছি না?

কারণ **Source Restrict করলেই আপনার সবচেয়ে বড় Data Integrity Problem Solve হয়ে যায়।**

Destination Restrict করলে আপনি **Future Flexibility হারাবেন**, কিন্তু Source Restrict করলে **বর্তমান Bug বন্ধ হবে**।

এটা আমার মতে দীর্ঘমেয়াদে আরও ভালো Architecture।
